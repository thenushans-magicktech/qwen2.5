EXAMPLE A - medium room, L-shape
Room:
- Wall 1: 3600 mm, from the Wall 4 corner to the Wall 2 corner. WINDOW 1200-2400 (centre 1800). Free run 50-3550 (3500 mm).
- Wall 2: 3000 mm, from the Wall 1 corner to the Wall 3 corner. Solid. Free run 50-2950 (2900 mm). Hob centre range 1100-1900.
- Wall 3: 3600 mm, from the Wall 2 corner to the Wall 4 corner. DOOR 2500-3400 (near the Wall 4 corner). Free run 50-2400 (2350 mm).
- Wall 4: 3000 mm, from the Wall 3 corner to the Wall 1 corner. Solid. Free run 50-2950 (2900 mm). Hob centre range 1100-1900.
Designer thinking: The window is on Wall 1, so the wet zone and the sink go there, centred at 1800. Wall 2 and Wall 4 are solid, but the door is near the Wall 4 corner, so the hob goes on Wall 2, farther from the door: L-shape Wall 1 + Wall 2. Sink and hob are on different walls, about 2 m apart. The tall pantry stands at the free end of Wall 1 near the entrance side, away from the hob. Purifier beside the window. Drawers on the hob wall, wicker near the sink.

- Wall 4: 2700 mm, from the Wall 3 corner to the Wall 1 corner. DOOR 300-1200 (near the Wall 3 corner). Free run 1300-2650 (1350 mm).

EXAMPLE C - large room, U-shape
Room:
- Wall 1: 3600 mm, from the Wall 4 corner to the Wall 2 corner. Solid. Free run 50-3550 (3500 mm). Hob centre range 1100-2450.
- Wall 2: 4200 mm, from the Wall 1 corner to the Wall 3 corner. WINDOW 1500-2700 (centre 2100). Free run 50-4150 (4100 mm).
- Wall 3: 3600 mm, from the Wall 2 corner to the Wall 4 corner. Solid. Free run 50-3550 (3500 mm). Hob centre range 1100-2450.
- Wall 4: 4200 mm, from the Wall 3 corner to the Wall 1 corner. DOOR 300-1200 (near the Wall 3 corner). Free run 1300-4150 (2850 mm).
- U-shape on Wall 2: aisle between the Wall 1 and Wall 3 runs would be 3080 mm (needs 1200+).
Designer thinking: A large room with the window centred on Wall 2: SINK-1050 at 2100. The door is near the Wall 3 corner, so the hob goes on Wall 1 (farther from the door) and Wall 3 becomes the storage wall near the entrance: U-shape. Hob at 1700 on Wall 1 with the spice pull-out and an LPG unit beside it. Oven tall unit and pantry at the free end of Wall 3 by the entrance. Purifier beside the window, one glass display unit to finish a run.
